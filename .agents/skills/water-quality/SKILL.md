# AquaSense Water Quality and Sensors

Trace the relevant value from sensor availability and raw reading through ADC
voltage, divider compensation, calibration, filtering, validation, persistence,
and display. Verify the selected device and calibration evidence. Distinguish
physical sensors from software fields; include dissolved oxygen only when the
path provides it. ORP is not direct dissolved oxygen, pH and temperature alone
do not measure total ammonia, and raw turbidity ADC is not NTU without a
defensible calibration.

Do not invent coefficients. Identify trusted versus provisional values and give
repeatable calibration or verification steps where needed.
