# AquaSense Hardware Review

Use available wiring diagrams, photos, current pin configuration, selected
firmware target, and user measurements. Verify board and sensor variants,
display, GPIO, dividers, supply rails, grounds, relay polarity, inductive-load
protection, and physical construction for the specific setup. Treat remembered
or documented values as claims to verify and surface conflicts. Do not assume
undocumented parts or approve a circuit from continuity alone.

Separate confirmed issues from likely concerns and items requiring measurement.
Explain the electrical reason and offer practical meter or inspection steps.
Do not claim physical conditions that the supplied evidence cannot establish.
